    CALQARE may monitor and investigate suspected violations. Depending on severity, actions may include written warning, removal from live discussion, temporary suspension, or permanent termination of Hub access without refund.

    • Unlimited access continues only while no Section 7 violation has been committed. A violation may result in immediate termination without refund and a ban from all CALQARE services and products.

    9 Digital Nature of the Program

    The program is a digitally delivered educational service (lectures, recordings, reviewers, PDFs, exercises, links, and other online materials).
